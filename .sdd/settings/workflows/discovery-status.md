# Workflow: discovery-status

1. Follow `_common.md` startup rules and read `discovery.json` plus only the headings/state needed from the discovery artifacts.
2. Report the current phase, iteration, selected hypothesis, market verdict, business verdict, product-outline state, Apple/Google/cross-platform Store Design Readiness scores, hard flags, decision, promotion eligibility, constraints, and unresolved blockers or conditions.
3. Identify the next valid discovery command. If complete with `go`, distinguish eligibility from explicit approval to run `spec-init`.
4. This workflow is read-only. Do not browse, edit artifacts, or change lifecycle state.
