# Workflow: validate-gap

1. Follow `_common.md`; load approved requirements and the gap-analysis rule.
2. Inspect the live codebase, tests, schemas, APIs, environment configuration, and platform contracts relevant to the spec.
3. Produce `.sdd/specs/<feature>/gap-analysis.md` with current assets, missing capabilities, constraints, options, recommendation, effort, risk, and research items.
4. Prefer a recommendation while preserving credible alternatives and trade-offs.
5. Do not change artifact approvals or advance the lifecycle.
