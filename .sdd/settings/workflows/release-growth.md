# Workflow: release-growth

1. Follow `_common.md`, `release-principles.md`, `release-marketing.md`, and `release-measurement-growth.md`; load the latest metric snapshots, experiment history, research, budget/runway, developer capacity, product backlog, support/reliability state, and cohort economics.
2. Diagnose the primary constrained stage with counts, rates, segments, uncertainty, and alternative explanations. If data quality or sample size is inadequate, fix measurement or wait; do not manufacture a growth recommendation.
3. Create or update `growth-plan.md`: prioritized hypotheses, one main variable per experiment, audience/variant, primary metric, guardrails, minimum run/sample, cost/capacity, and scale/iterate/stop rules. Favor product value, retention, reliability, and conversion bottlenecks before more acquisition.
4. Refresh mutable channel/store/pricing/policy research before experiments that depend on it and append sources. Any implementation change returns to SDD; release-growth may open/recommend a spec but never bypass requirements/design/tasks/implementation approval.
5. Classify experiment rigor as `directional`, `controlled`, or `high-risk`. Persist owner, release/candidate link, eligibility, exposure unit, assignment method, control/variant identity, primary metric, guardrails, minimum run/sample, stop rule, and decision rule.
6. Require explicit approval before spend, price changes, public outreach/content, store-page publication, messaging, or production experiments. Execute only the authorized experiment and append its schema-valid immutable setup and result to `experiments.jsonl`.
7. Classify results as `win`, `loss`, `inconclusive`, or `invalid`; preserve losses. Route every learning to `release`, `marketing-assets`, `sdd`, `discovery`, or `steering-index`. Product behavior returns to SDD; invalidated ICP, problem, value proposition, willingness to pay, or business economics returns to discovery.
8. Set growth to `learning`, `scaling`, `paused`, or `complete`, update history, and run SDD lint. Scaling requires downstream value/contribution plus healthy guardrails and an incrementality rationale. Completion additionally requires no active experiment/spend, reconciled budget, dispositions for alerts, and owned learning destinations.
