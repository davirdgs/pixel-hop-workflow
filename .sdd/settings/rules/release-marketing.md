# Release Marketing and Channel Selection

## Start from economics

Confirm audience, market, job, positioning, release goal, budget/currency, time horizon, creative/support capacity, price, store/payment/tax/refund assumptions, net contribution or LTV range, payback limit, and maximum sustainable CAC/CPA/CPI. If LTV is immature, use conservative contribution windows and cap learning spend.

## Research reachable channels

Evaluate relevant organic and paid options, including store search/ASO, owned landing/email/content, communities, creators, editorial/PR, referral/share loops, partnerships, Apple Ads, Google App campaigns, and audience-fit social/video networks. For every candidate record:

- audience intent, geography/platform availability, message/creative fit, targeting/privacy constraints, minimum viable creative and operational load;
- current pricing/bidding model and independent benchmark range with market/category/sample caveats;
- conversion and value event support, attribution/reporting limits, expected learning volume, landing/custom-store-page support, and incrementality options;
- bounded test budget/time, expected downside/base/upside value, stop rule, and opportunity cost.

Do not spread a small budget across channels that cannot individually reach a learning threshold. Prefer one or two measurable hypotheses, message-matched store pages, and creative variants derived from different audience promises. Reserve budget for iteration rather than committing it all to the first creative.

## Organic is still an experiment

Account for founder time, content cadence, moderation/support burden, community rules, creator compensation, editorial lead time, localization, reuse, and attribution. Select tactics that can be sustained after launch; a one-week content burst is not a channel strategy.

## Decisions

Scale only after sufficient downstream activation/retention/revenue or contribution evidence and healthy guardrails. Iterate when the audience/channel shows qualified signal but message, page, or activation is the constraint. Stop when spend/time caps, data-quality failures, negative contribution, low-quality cohorts, saturation, policy risk, or support/reliability guardrails are breached.
